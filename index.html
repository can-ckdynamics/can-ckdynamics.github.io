AI-ACCELERATED UAV ENGINEERING

Founded in February 2026 in Istanbul, CK Dynamics combines composite additive manufacturing with AI-driven flight software engineering. Our engineering pipeline leverages the Anthropic Claude API to analyze IMU and optical-flow telemetry logs, synthesize parametric Python CAD scripts for structural weight reduction, and accelerate embedded C++, ROS2, and MAVLink autonomy development.
